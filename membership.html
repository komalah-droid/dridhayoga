Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need To Know

Can I freeze or cancel my Monthly Membership?

Yes. You may freeze your membership up to 60 days per year with 7 days notice, or cancel anytime before your next monthly renewal without penalties.

What should I wear and bring for my first session?

Wear breathable, flexible clothing (linen or athletic wear). We provide premium Manduka mats, blocks, blankets, and towels. Bring a reusable water bottle.

Are these classes suitable if I am completely new or pregnant?

Yes! Our Gentle Morning Flow, Restorative Candlelight, and Sound Baths are deeply nurturing for all levels. Please notify your instructor before class so we can offer personalized prop modifications.
